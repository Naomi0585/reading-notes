# AI and Prompt Engineering 

## Prompt: 

Teach me how to change the oil in my car. Make it easy to follow but still effective. 

## Output 

Sure! Here’s a simple, step-by-step guide that’s easy to follow but still does the job right. You don’t need to be a mechanic—just take your time.

<u>🛢️ How to Change Your Car’s Oil (Beginner-Friendly)</u>
🔧 What You’ll Need

* New engine oil (correct type & amount — check your owner’s manual)
* New oil filter
* Oil filter wrench
* Socket wrench (for the drain plug)
* Oil drain pan
* Funnel
* Gloves & paper towels/rags
* Jack and jack stands or ramps (if needed)

<u>🚗 Step 1: Prepare the Car</u>

* Park on level ground
* Turn the engine off and let it cool for ~10 minutes
(Warm oil drains better, hot oil burns!)
* Open the hood and remove the oil cap (helps oil drain faster)

<u>🔼 Step 2: Raise the Car (If Needed)</u>


* Use ramps or a jack + jack stands
* Never rely on the jack alone

<u>🪛 Step 3: Drain the Old Oil</U>

* Slide the drain pan under the oil pan
* Use a socket wrench to loosen the drain plug
* Remove the plug by hand and let oil drain completely
(This takes 5–10 minutes)
* Put the drain plug back in and tighten snug—not super tight

<u>🧴 Step 4: Remove & Replace the Oil Filter</u>

* Find the oil filter (cylinder-shaped)
* Use the oil filter wrench to loosen it
* Expect some oil to drip—this is normal
* Lightly rub new oil on the rubber gasket of the new filter
* Screw on the new filter hand-tight only

<u>🪣 Step 5: Add New Oil</u>

* Insert funnel into oil fill hole
* Pour in most (not all) of the recommended oil
* Replace oil cap

<u>🚘 Step 6: Start & Check</u>

* Start engine and let it run for 30–60 seconds
* Turn engine off and wait 2 minutes
* Check oil level with the dipstick
* Add oil if needed (don’t overfill!)

<u>🔍 Step 7: Final Check</u>

* Look under the car for leaks
* Make sure drain plug and filter are secure

<u>♻️ Step 8: Dispose of Old Oil Properly</u>

* Pour old oil into the empty oil container
* Take it to an auto parts store or recycling center
* Never dump oil in the trash or ground

### ✅ Pro Tips

* Change oil every 3,000–5,000 miles (or as manual says)
* Write down the mileage/date
* If unsure about oil type: check the oil cap or manual
